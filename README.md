# Super-Store-Analysis

## Objective 
To dig into Superstore’s data and uncover what’s really behind its performance.
from the products and customers driving sales to the regions, city and trends shaping its profitability.

## Process 
- I started by using Power Query to clean and prepare the raw data. 
- I then used DAX to build the KPIs and measures needed for the analysis. 
- Finally, I used Power BI to explore the data, uncover key patterns, and bring the insights to life through an interactive dashboard.

## KPIs 
- Revenue 
- Profit 
- Cost 
- Discount 
- Orders 
- Sales 
- Customers 
- Top selling Products 
- Most profitable City

## Key Insights 
-  A significant portion of the stores potential profit is reduced through heavy discounting strategies.
- The analysis suggest that profitability could improve if discount are better optimized and strategically controlled.
- William Brown placed more orders than any other customer, making him the store's most active customer by order count.
- New York City recorded the highest number of customers compared to all other cities, showing a strong customer concentration in the market.
- The standard Class shipping mode is the most preferred delivery option preference for cost-effective shipping.
- Sean Miller generated the highest revenue also receiving the highest discount, highlighting the impact of high-value customers on overall performance.
- The store generated its highest revenue from chairs, slightly outperforming phones as the top revenue generating products.
- Binders recorded the highest sales volume among all products, making them the most frequent purchase item in the store.
